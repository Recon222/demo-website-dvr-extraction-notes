## 3. Deviations, with evidence

### D-1. The phone's `failed` trio is **not** ported

Plan §5 lists all four. The demo has three HUD states (R-2). `SCANNER_COLORS` is
`satisfies Record<BootHudState, ScannerStateColors>` — a fourth key would not typecheck without
widening the key type, which would also destroy the totality guard `SplashScreen`'s `statusBody`
depends on (the R-9 fix: adding a member yields exactly one `TS2741`). The phone's values are in
the module docblock with their `file:line` so the day a failure arm exists it is one line.

### D-2. The SKIP pill takes `overlay`, not `scrim`

Plan §5's row names the scrim token. Measured, SKIP label over pill fill over frame:

| Frame | `scrim` 0.32 | shipped `rgba(4,8,14,0.55)` | **`overlay` 0.9** |
|---|---|---|---|
| white | **1.02** | 2.07 | **4.80** |
| mid grey | 2.72 | 4.66 | 5.58 |
| black | 8.02 | 8.32 | 6.50 |
| the gate itself | 6.17 | 7.52 | 6.17 |

The pill sits at `zIndex: 2` over an intro video of unknown content (`BOOT_VIDEO`, D7's
drop-in). That is precisely the case U4.4 carved `MEDIA_CLOSE_CHIP` and `PDF_LOADING_SCRIM` out
of `scrim` for, at `overlay`'s exact value, and `palette-contrast.test.ts` rows 36-37b already
pin the identical regression on the identical token for the media chip. `scrim` would ship the
pill **worse than the literal it replaces**. `overlay` is a token, so §111's exemption list is
unaffected. Probe P4 kills the plan's version.

### D-3. The v1 disclosure pin is **replaced**, not raised

Plan §5 says raise the floor. The floor cannot see the defect (probes P1a/P1b). The claim splits:
`SplashScreen.test.tsx` pins the **paint** (rendered colour === the constant),
`palette-contrast.test.ts` pins the **ratio** (composited, ≥ 4.5, against the imported ground).
Alpha chosen 0.80 → 5.19 rather than the ~0.815 that would reproduce v1's 5.27 exactly: 5.27 was
an outcome on a ground that no longer exists, and the stated intent was *"comfortably over 4.5,
still visually subordinate"* — 5.19 against the HUD's 7.30 is that.

### D-4. New shared test helper `ui/__tests__/jsdom-colour.ts` (two callers)

Three private `hexToJsdomRgb` copies already exist and all three are hex-only; a token layer
deriving alphas with `withAlpha` produces `rgba()` constants they throw on. Folding the three in
would mean editing files three other rows own — proposed as a deferral instead (§5).

## 5. Deferral proposals (`dt-review-aggregator` decides and writes)

### Proposal 1 — the three private `hexToJsdomRgb` copies

- **Source:** U8.1 report §3 D-4. `inputs/__tests__/CoordinateDisplay.test.tsx:8`,
  `screens/__tests__/DeleteConfirmationModal.test.tsx:164`,
  `screens/__tests__/Toggle.test.tsx:29`.
- **What:** three byte-similar hex-only copies of jsdom's colour normalisation, now four
  implementations counting `ui/__tests__/jsdom-colour.ts`. All three throw on the `rgba()`
  constants a `withAlpha`-derived token layer produces, so each will be re-written the first
  time its file pins a derived alpha.
- **Why deferred:** the three files belong to A74/A75/A81's rows, not U8.1's, and W4 runs three
  packages in parallel across a frozen file set. Editing them from here is the cross-package
  collision §6.2 exists to prevent, for a test-only tidy-up.
- **Trigger:** the next package that opens any one of those three files re-points it at
  `ui/__tests__/jsdom-colour.ts` and deletes its private copy. Observable as
  `git grep -c "hexToJsdomRgb" features/demo` returning more than 0 after W4 merges.

### Proposal 2 — the SKIP pill's border is a sole boundary at 1.87

- **Source:** U8.1, `scanner-hud-colors.ts` `SCANNER_SKIP_PILL.border`.
- **What:** `withAlpha(primary, 0.45)` measures **1.87** on the gate ground (1.91 before D8
  lightened it), and D8 also makes the pill's *fill* invisible against the gate — over the HUD
  the border is the pill's only boundary. §C.3 rule 4 says a border is decorative only when the
  fill, the icon and the text each carry the meaning independently; here the label carries it
  alone, at 6.17.
- **Why deferred:** the value is pre-existing and moved by 0.04, not introduced; SKIP is a
  demo-only control the phone does not have (§87), so there is no phone value to port and any
  fix is an invention; and it sits inside the §118 input-boundary family, which is already
  ledgered as phone-verbatim and accepted. Fixing it here would mean inventing a boundary
  treatment for a control on the surface D1's owner device-pass is about to look at.
- **Trigger:** the **D1 owner device-pass at U8 exit** (GATES §13, "final side-by-side"). If the
  owner reads the pill as ill-defined against the gate, the boundary takes a value that clears
  3.0 — measured, `colors.borderLight #2e5f97` does **not** (2.24); `colors.primary` at full
  strength does, at **3.94**, i.e. dropping the 0.45 alpha rather than changing the hue. If the
  owner does not raise it, this closes as accepted under §118 with the 1.87 recorded.

## 7. Two likely defects (what I would look at first if this comes back)

**(a) The gate now reads flatter than it did, and a ratio cannot see that.** The ground moved
from near-black to the same navy family as the HUD's own glow, and the glow fill deepened at the
same time. Every number improved; the *separation* between the frame's halo and the ground it
sits on did not necessarily. This is the exact defect class D1 exists for (matrix row 33's ΔE
argument, and PR #125's two device-pass findings). The contrast rows here are ratio-only — I did
**not** add a ΔE bound between `glow` and `SCANNER_GROUND`, because the phone has none and
inventing a bound for a decorative halo would be building a number to defend a guess.

Measured (CIE76, glow flattened over the ground), because the direction is not obvious:

| Glow | Flattens to | ΔE from the ground |
|---|---|---|
| the retired `rgba(43,140,193,0.14)` over the retired `#000314` | — | **10.88** |
| `idle` `rgba(43,140,193,0.25)` | `rgb(11, 65, 111)` | **11.46** |
| `scanning` `rgba(43,140,193,0.35)` | `rgb(15, 75, 122)` | **15.86** |
| `authorized` `rgba(16,209,119,0.35)` | `rgb(6, 99, 96)` | **44.75** |

So the halo did **not** flatten — it separates further than it used to at every state. Row 33's
3–12 band is a `recessed`-tier bound and does not govern a decorative halo, but `scanning`'s
15.86 sits above its upper "too deep" edge, which is worth the owner's eye rather than a pin.
The risk I would actually look for at the U8-exit pass is the reverse of flatness: on the lighter
ground the scanning glow may now read as a visible rectangle rather than a diffuse halo.

**(b) The header label no longer distinguishes the gate from the rest of the demo's chrome.**
`#99badd` at 18px is also what half the demo's secondary text is. On the phone BIOMETRIC LOCK is
accent-coloured and unmistakably a *lock* screen; here it now reads as ordinary muted chrome
with a flicker animation. §89 leaves no alternative at 18px — the accent is 3.94 — but if the
owner wants the header to carry the accent back, the honest route is size, not colour: 24px makes
it WCAG large text and `primary`'s 3.94 legal. That is a type-scale change (24 **is** on §4.9's
ramp), so it is a decision, not a fix, and I have not taken it.
