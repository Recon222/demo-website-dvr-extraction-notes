### Commit `b383a30` — `test(u0.5): port the phone's palette contrast contract, both schemes`

New `features/demo/ui/__tests__/palette-contrast.test.ts` (447 lines). 4 live cases + 15 `it.todo`.

**Design decision 1 — the compositor is U0.2's production `flattenOver`, not a private copy.**
The plan says so three times: U0.2's row (*"Signatures, exact — U0.5 consumes both from a different
package"*), §C.4 (*"What ports: … `flattenOver` (A53)"*), and the U0.5 row itself. *Rejected
alternative:* port the phone's own local `over`/`flatten` (`:57-65`, `:121-128`), which is what the
phone does and what the partner legwork §4 item 5 flagged as an open choice. Rejected because a private
copy leaves the helper every later recipe composites with unexercised by any gate — the same tautology
the phone's deep-import rule (`:24-29`) exists to prevent. Probe 3 proves the coupling is real.

Two seams between the two implementations, both documented in the source:

- `flattenOver` **rounds** each channel per fold step (`scale.ts:173`); the phone's `over` keeps floats.
  Under 1/255 per channel, < 0.01 on a ratio, and rounding is what an 8-bit screen does. The three
  sanity pairs composite nothing, so they are exact either way.
- `flattenOver` **returns its input unchanged** when a layer is unparseable — deliberate in production
  (`transparent` and named colours stay safe), poison in a contrast test. `flatten()` therefore opens
  with `stack.forEach(parse)`, which throws. See commit 3; this is where the one SURVIVED probe landed.

**Design decision 2 — the ground stacks do not land in a degraded form.** *Rejected alternative:*
`DARK_GROUNDS = [DARK_BG]` alone, so rows 4/5/8/10 could measure the text ramp on the flat app
background today and U1.1 would extend the array. Rejected because a row titled "on every glass tier"
that measures one flat hex is green and lying, and if U1.1 forgot to extend it, it would stay that way
forever. `it.todo` is loud; a partial pin is silent.

**Design decision 3 — one demo-side addition: `sanity-checks the compositing, distance and collection
helpers`.** For an entire phase, 15 of 19 cases here are todo, so nothing else stands between a broken
helper and a wave of silently-wrong un-todos at U1.1. It pins the 3-deep fold identity, `parse`
rejecting `color-mix()` / a gradient / 3-digit hex, `deltaE(black, white) === 100` and `deltaE(x,x)
=== 0`, `stops()`'s two-stack split with its `under` tail, `worst` as a minimum, and `offenders`'
threshold in both directions. *Rejected alternative:* ship only what the four live rows use and let
U1.1 add `deltaE`/`stops`/`worst`/`offenders`. Rejected because re-deriving CIE76 by hand later is a
transcription risk on the one helper the contrast ratio is blind to, and U1.1's own Tests column says
its job is to UN-TODO, not to build. Probe 3 and probe 9 both land in this block.

**Deep imports are preserved as a rule, not just copied.** The CTA stops are read off `GLASS.accentFrom`
/ `GLASS.accentTo`; the palette rows off `palette.light` / `palette.dark`. Nothing is retyped. Where a
constant does not exist yet, the row is a todo naming its owner — never a literal typed in here.

## 9. Successor notes — invariants of `palette-contrast.test.ts` and the two literal guards

1. **The two ground stacks land in U1.1, verbatim from the comment at the point of use.** Three
   asymmetries are not typos: `nestedCard` sits on `card`'s **LOWER** stop, `recessed` sits on `sheet`'s

2. **`flattenOver` treats its LAST ground as opaque and discards its alpha.** Both stacks must bottom
   out at `background`, never at a glass stop, or the answer is silently wrong rather than an error.
3. **`flatten()` opens with `stack.forEach(parse)` and that line is load-bearing.** Delete it and an
   unparseable layer *buried* in a stack makes `flattenOver` return the top colour unchanged — a
   confident wrong ratio. The 1-deep and top-of-stack cases cannot see it (probes 4, 4b, 4c). If you
   add a pin for it, bury the bad layer.
4. **Light `textTertiary` pins at 3.87, not the phone's 3.79.** The phone holds BOTH themes to the dark
   number and its own comment records ~0.08 of unasserted light slack as DEF-063's open owner question.
   The plan closes that gap on the demo side. Do not copy `>= 3.79` for light.
5. **`recessed` is ΔE, two-sided (3..12), PER STOP. `nestedCard` is a CONTRAST bound at 1.25.** `Math.max`
   across stops is the bug the phone already shipped once: the lower bound then only fires when BOTH
   stops go flat.
6. **Every `it.todo` title carries its §C.1 row number(s) and its owning package.** Un-todoing means
   deleting `.todo` and writing the body from the phone's cited lines — never inventing a bound, and
   never typing a constant the owning package has not created yet (that is the `DangerFill` tautology,
   phone `:265-274`).

## 10. My own two most likely defects

**(a) The live surface is thin and the todos carry the weight — and nothing mechanically enforces
their retirement.** 15 of 19 cases are `it.todo`, so §6.6 gate 2 (*"the ported contrast test — green"*)
is satisfied today by a file that measures nine pairs. If U1.1 / U3.1 / U4.4 / U5.2 un-todo carelessly
— or simply forget — the gate stays green over a contract that never came online, which is precisely
the class of failure this file was ported to prevent. Mitigations in place: each todo names its row and
its owner in the title, U1.1's own Tests column says "UN-TODO", and §8 item 6 asks for the gate wording
to be tightened. I deliberately did **not** add a "there are exactly 15 todos" assertion — that is a
change-detector that fails on every legitimate un-todo and teaches people to edit the number. **If a
reviewer wants a mechanical backstop, the honest shape is a phase-exit check that the todo set is a
subset of the not-yet-merged packages**, and that belongs in the verification lane, not in this file.

**(b) The `flattenOver` rounding seam is unmeasured on a composited row.** Every live row is flat and
opaque, so the nine measured pairs are exact. Every **todo** row composites, and `flattenOver` rounds
each channel per fold step while the phone's `over` keeps floats. The margins look comfortable — row
33's dark lower stop measured 4.0 against a lower bound of 3, and a 1/255 channel error is ~0.3 ΔE —
but **nobody has yet run a composited row through this helper and compared it to the phone's own
number**, because that only becomes possible when U1.1 lands the tiers. If U1.1 sees a ΔE or a ratio
within ~0.5 of a bound, **the first hypothesis should be this rounding seam, not the tier values.** The
alternative (porting the phone's float `over`) was rejected on the plan's explicit instruction and is
recorded in commit `b383a30`'s body; it remains the fallback if the seam proves to matter.
